# Stocx — US Market Dashboard & Command Center

Following the visual refresh and TradingView workspace integration, Stocx now features a unified **US Market Dashboard**—serving as a high-density command center for tracking macroeconomic breadth, benchmark indices, and intraday equity momentum.

---

## 1. Unified Market Overview & Index Telemetry

- **Benchmark Index Cards**: Real-time snapshot cards for **S&P 500 (`SPX`)**, **Nasdaq Composite (`IXIC`)**, **Dow Jones Industrial Average (`DJI`)**, and **Russell 2000 (`RUT`)** featuring intraday mini-sparklines, day ranges, and net change metrics.
- **Macro & Volatility Pulse**: Instant tracking of systemic risk indicators, including the **CBOE Volatility Index (`VIX`)**, the **10-Year Treasury Yield (`US10Y`)**, and the **US Dollar Index (`DXY`)**.
- **Market Breadth Gauge**: An advance-decline ratio and cumulative volume monitor measuring whether current index movements are supported by broad market participation.

---

## 2. Sector Performance & Capital Flow

- **Dynamic Sector Heat Map**: Real-time breakdown of all 11 GICS sectors (Technology, Healthcare, Energy, Financials, Consumer Discretionary, etc.) sorted by relative strength.
- **Capital Rotation Insights**: Clear visual identifiers highlighting defensive vs. cyclical asset flows across the trading day.

---

## 3. Real-Time Market Movers

- **Top Gainers & Decliners**: Low-latency screener tracking highest percentage movers with volume-relative metrics and market cap filtering.
- **Unusual Volume & Most Active**: Surfaces tickers experiencing institutional accumulation or distribution spikes relative to their 30-day average daily volume.
- **Fast-Action Watchlist**: Pin, filter, and track custom equities directly from the dashboard with one-click transitions to chart analysis.

---

## 4. Next on the Roadmap

With the **Markets** split workspace and **Dashboard** command center fully operational, upcoming updates will complete the core Stocx experience:

- **Dedicated Stock View (`/stock/[symbol]`)**: Deep-dive individual asset workspace featuring granular financial statements, analyst consensus targets, SEC filings, and order flow metrics.
- **User Profile & Portfolio Suite (`/profile`)**: Personalized watchlist management, paper trading portfolio tracking, custom alert thresholds, and multi-brokerage sync.
- **AI Market Insights**: Machine-learning summarization of market-moving catalysts, earnings call transcript sentiment analysis, and intelligent automated technical setups.
