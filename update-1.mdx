# Stocx — Visual Refresh & TradingView Integration

Stocx has been updated with a refined, distraction-free visual design focused on answering **"What is happening in markets?" in seconds**.

---

## 1. Editorial Interface & Split Workspace

- **Dual-Pane Layout**: A 50/50 split screen with an editorial asset list on the left and a contextual analysis panel on the right.
- **Monochrome Theme**: Deep near-black surfaces (`#090909`) on charcoal (`#151515`) with hairline dividers (`#1C1C1F`).
- **Restrained Accents**: Subtle institutional green (`#2ebd85`) and red (`#f6465d`) instead of oversaturated glows.
- **Active Asset Indicator**: Selected assets are highlighted by a crisp vertical white bar on the edge of the row.

---

## 2. Integrated TradingView Widgets

The contextual detail panel now embeds six core TradingView widgets configured for dark mode:

### 1. Multi-Mode Price Charts

Switch between three dedicated TradingView chart views in real time:

- **Interactive Candlestick Chart**: Full candle charting with volume and grid lines.
- **Symbol Area Overview**: Clean gradient line view for quick trend assessment.
- **Mini Overview**: Ultra-minimalist 12-month performance glance.

### 2. Technical Analysis Speedometer

- Real-time gauge combining oscillators and moving averages to display an instant **Buy / Neutral / Sell** consensus for the selected asset.

### 3. Global Stock Heatmap

- Visual equity map grouped by sector and sized by market capitalization to instantly pinpoint where capital is flowing.

### 4. Forex Cross Rates Matrix

- Comprehensive currency matrix tracking real-time cross rates and daily percentage changes across major global pairs.

### 5. Economic Calendar

- Live schedule of high-impact global macroeconomic releases, central bank rate decisions, and inflation prints.

### 6. Market Ticker Tape

- Continuous real-time strip tracking primary benchmark indices across US, European, and Asian sessions.
